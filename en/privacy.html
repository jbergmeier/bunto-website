Privacy Policy

In short: The app Bunto does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no account, no analytics or tracking, and no network access.

1. Controller

Bergmeier&Bergmeier GbR, Christinenweg 25, 59069 Hamm, Germany · Email: info@smaflex.com

2. The “Bunto” app

  • No data collection: The app collects no data and sends nothing to us or to third parties. It contains no third-party SDKs.
  • No ads, no purchases: No advertising IDs are used and there are no payment features.
  • Local storage: Coloring progress is stored on the device only and never leaves it. It is removed when the app is deleted.
  • Photos (optional): If someone taps “Save to Photos”, iOS asks for write-only access to Photos. The app does not read existing photos.

3. This website

This website uses no cookies, no tracking, no analytics, no fonts or scripts from third-party servers and embeds no external content. When you visit, your browser technically transmits data (e.g. IP address, time, requested page) to the server. Our web server does not write these access data to log files; the server operator or hosting provider may process them briefly to ensure operation and security (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. Contact

If you email us, we process your details only to answer your request and delete them once no longer needed.

5. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
