For parents 👨‍👩‍👧

  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription – neither now nor later.
  • No data collection: Bunto does not collect, store or transmit personal data. There are no third-party SDKs.
  • Offline: The app needs no internet access and has no outbound links.
  • Photos: Only when you or your child tap “Save”, iOS asks for write-only access to Photos. Bunto never reads your photos.
  • Built for the App Store Kids Category, age rating 4+.
